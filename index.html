Standing Orders

The rules that keep Comrades running. Read them once, live by them always.

01

No cheating, no exploiting

Hacking, scripting, duping, and known exploit abuse are permanent-ban offenses. Reports are investigated with server logs, not accusations.

02

KOS is the law of the land

This is a hardcore PvP server. Kill on sight is allowed everywhere outside of designated safe zones. Come armed or come careful.

03

No racism, no harassment

In-game or in Discord — slurs, hate speech, and targeted harassment result in an immediate ban. This applies in text, voice, and base names.

04

Report bugs, don't abuse them

Found a dupe, a wall glitch, or something that feels broken? Report it to an admin. Using it first and reporting it later is still an exploit.

05

Admin calls are final

Admins act on log evidence and community reports. Disputes go through Discord tickets — not in-game arguments.
